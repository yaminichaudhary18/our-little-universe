To my bby darling,

Hie bby, mie luksii, my nony, bby darling, sweetie pie, mie buggu… 🥹❤️ You have so many names because one name could never hold all the love I have for you. And just think—our little story started at school, and somehow you became one of the most special and best part of my life. 💗 When someone asks me to choose my favourite memory with you, I genuinely can’t pick just one. It’s every little moment, every laugh, every conversation, and even the ordinary days that feel special simply because I spent them with you. You make so many tiny moments worth keeping forever. 🫂✨ I know we’re not perfect bby we fight alot and sometimes talk about breakup but its temporary and the love in our hearts for eac other is permanent. We can have our silly fights and misunderstandings, but I hope we keep learning, listening, forgiving, and choosing each other with love. I want more laughs, more hugs, more random talks, more memories—and, of course, more chances to annoy you and steal all your attention. 😭😂💋 And when I think about my future, I really do hope you’re in it. I dream of us growing together, supporting each other’s dreams, and one day building a little life of our own. The thought of getting to call you my husband someday makes me smile. I don’t expect every day to be perfect; I just hope we keep putting care and effort into us, one day at a time. 🥹🤍 Thank you for being my bby, my sweetie pie, my favourite person, and my mie buggu. If I could go back to the beginning, I’d still want our story to start—and I’d still want to see where it takes us. I love you more than this little letter can explain, and I’m so excited for all the memories we haven’t made yet. Congratulations bby we completed two years together and moving towards for staying together forever. ❤️🧿♾️
